# Voiceover script — six clips, read by a text-to-speech voice

Written to be read aloud: no abbreviations, numbers as they should be said, the site name said "everyone's dot lol". "GTA" and "AI" stay as capitals; ElevenLabs reads them as letters (check the first render; if not, write "G T A" and "A I"). Timestamps are targets for the cut; the caption timings come from the audio length. Hook lines are twelve words or fewer. The hook is on screen in frame one and spoken at zero seconds. Real builds take about two minutes; never fake the timing, cut with the card.

Words in brackets are stage directions, not spoken. Lines marked SILENT have no voice; the captions do the talking.
