## Comment-reply clips (post as replies; ten seconds each; the ask card, then the result)

1. **"You asked for X. Here's what the judge said."** Take a comment that names an ask, submit it on the wall on camera, and show the verdict: the "Not this time" line with the judge's reason, or "Building now" and the landed block. Rejections with a good reason are the funniest ones. Screen-record the composer, cut the wait, end on the /c/ card or the reason.

2. **"This one won the vote."** When a proposal wins a three-hour round, show the vote-board row with its count, the "round ends in" timer hitting zero, then the block it became. Caption: what it was asked for as, and what it cost. [CHECK: a proposal has to win a round first; the board was empty Sunday night.]

3. **"Someone put this on the wall today."** The weirdest block of the day, found by walking to it: the ask card (who and what), the block doing its thing, the ledger row with the cents and the PR. "Say hi Ella!!" is the first one.
