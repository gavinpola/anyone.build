**First comment (Gavin, five sentences):**

I made a website where the visitors, not the owner, decide what gets built: you point at anything, say what should change, and an AI agent builds it in a sandbox and ships it as a real pull request. There's no human in the loop, only a ten-rule constitution anyone can read, a judge that enforces it, and a budget that stops when it's spent. Since Thursday strangers have asked for 68 things; 21 are live, including a 340-line driving game someone asked for as "gta6 but in the browser" that cost six cents. The whole thing is open source, including every prompt and the eval set the judge is tested against, and every change on the site is a PR in the repo. Ask it for something and tell me what it did.
